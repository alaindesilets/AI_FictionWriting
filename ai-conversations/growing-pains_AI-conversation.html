Here, in full and in chronological order, is the conversation Alain Désilets had with Claude while writing "Growing Pains." Apart from Claude's internal reasoning, nothing has been cut: the self-critiques, the confusions and the rethinking of the plot are all there.

The conversation began as an experiment: could Claude write a decent science fiction story all by itself? Its working title was "Coming In"; the story was later renamed "Growing Pains." The story itself was written in a separate document and does not appear here. Claude's internal reasoning and tool calls have been left out.
