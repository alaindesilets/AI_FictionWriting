AI Story Writing Prompt
You are going to write a short story (approximately 10 pages, 
2500-3000 words) whose working title is: "Memoirs of a Very Old Man".

In the project, you have access to the following documents:

- story-premiss.md: the premise of the story, as conceived by Alain Désilets
- style-guidelines.md: the stylistic principles to follow, which reflect Alain’s writing style
- a series of PDF files, each containing a short story that provides a sample of stories previously written by Alain. You are to imitate that style. Note that some of those stories may be incomplete.  Don't look at the **translations** folder!! These are translations of a story originally written in the other language. So their style may not be perfectly native, as it may have been influenced by the source language version.

Read all documents carefully before writing anything.

Your process:

Create two independent adversarial agents, that will collaborate to write the story
One agent (called the writer) writes, the other one (called the critic) critiques the work
To make the agents more independent of each other, make sure they don’t share the same memory.
Both agents should read and internalize the premise, guidelines, and style examples
The writer starts the process by writing a detailed synopsis for the story.
It then asks the critic to critique it and suggest improvements.
The two agents go back and forth, until they both feel the synopsis is good enough to start writing out the story.
The write writes a complete first draf and submits it to the critic.
The critic reviews it and suggest improvements.
And so on, until the story is deemed complete.
Allocate one hour for the whole effort.
Make sure you have something complete and presentable by the end of the hour.

- Work autonomously. Do not ask for feedback or clarification.
- Do not summarize what you are doing. Just do it.
